# Next Week Room

28h / 40h planner: FamilyMart + HSTEC, any 7 days. Live: https://hengvichet123.github.io/week-room/

- Weeks follow the real date: this week (Mon of today), next week, then the planning week.
- **FamilyMart** hours come from `roster.json` (`"YYYY-MM-DD": hours`, shift counted on its start date).
  Updated by Claude from the Google Calendar "バイト" calendar (豊橋野依インター events) or from a pasted
  weekly schedule, then pushed. Unlock "Lock FamilyMart" to drag by hand (saved in this browser only).
- **HSTEC** hours are dragged in the page and saved per date in that browser's localStorage, so they stay
  when the weeks move on. Phone and Mac keep separate copies. Hours that should show everywhere can go in
  `roster.json` under `"hstec"`.
